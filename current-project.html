Three Threads

What the collection explores

Suspended Silence gathers poems around three central themes, each rooted in close observation of the natural world.

Theme One

Nature as beauty

Poems that linger in the simple presence of the natural world, attentive to color, form, and the quiet pleasure of noticing.

Theme Two

Nature triggers memories

Poems that use the natural world as a doorway into personal history, where a place or a season carries the weight of what came before.

Theme Three

A call to protect the natural world

Poems that turn outward, asking readers to consider what is at stake in the landscapes and creatures her collection observes so closely.

A Note on Timing
