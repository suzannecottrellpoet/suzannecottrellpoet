Honors and Awards

Over the course of her writing life, Suzanne Cottrell has placed in national and international poetry contests, studied with respected writers and naturalists, and seen her work recognized by editors and juried exhibitions. This page gathers that record.

Honorable Mention

Aged

Received an Honorable Mention in The Amy Woodward Fisher World Day of Poetry Contest for Free Verse, sponsored by the Poetry Scribes of Spokane, Washington. Published in "Muse-ings," their Poetry Contest Issue, October 2017 (not considered formally published, as stated by the Poetry Scribes).

"The concept is inventive and apt. The terms of description, which become terms of comparison, are strong and pertinent and effective. It's a poem that easily and unhesitatingly made the first cut and went into the Possible Winners' pile. Nicely done; congratulations on a successful and arresting work of art."

Victor Bobb, English Professor, Whitworth University

A Record Built Slowly

Recognition earned over years, not months

These honors span a decade of consistent work. Workshop study with writers such as Robin Wall Kimmerer, Alison Hawthorne Deming, and Elizabeth Rush has shaped Suzanne's approach to nonfiction, while contest placements and juried exhibitions reflect sustained attention to craft across both poetry and collaborative projects.
