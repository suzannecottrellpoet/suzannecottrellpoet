Poetry, Fiction, Personal Narratives, and Nature Writing

Suzanne Cottrell

Suzanne Cottrell writes from close attention: to a riverbank, a pattern in tree bark, a family story carried for years before it finds its shape on the page. Her poetry, fiction, and nonfiction move between the natural world and the quieter terrain of memory.

Since her first published story in 2016, her work has appeared in more than eighty literary journals and anthologies, earned recognition in national and international poetry contests, and found a home in collaborative art exhibitions and public readings.

Observation as Practice

Writing that begins with watching closely

Suzanne's nature writing grows out of direct, patient observation: light moving across a leaf, an insect pausing on a petal, bark weathering into a pattern over years. The photographs on this site are hers, taken in the same spirit of attention that shapes her poems and personal narratives.

From an Editor

On "Triple Falls Portrait"

"Your poem, Triple Falls Portrait, is now published on NatureWriting. The images in your poem work like a series of haiku to create a portrait of the falls, the forest, and the people who enjoy them. Your photo portrait adds a triple meaning to your poem of triple falls. Brilliant! Thanks for sharing your work with NatureWriting readers. I hope you'll keep NatureWriting in mind as you write."

Ron Harton, Editor, NatureWriting (2017)
