Writing

Suzanne Cottrell writes across poetry, short fiction, personal narratives, and nature writing. What follows is a selection, organized by form, meant to show the range of her work rather than its entirety. Her complete publication history lives on the Publications page.

Writing Inspired by Art

Collaborations with visual artists

Several of Suzanne's pieces began as responses to paintings and photographs, written in collaboration with the artists whose work inspired them.

Collaboration

Solitary Hunters

A collaborative project with plein air artist Jenny McKinnon Wright's painting "Hunting." The painting appeared on the cover of Remington Review, Summer 2022, with the painting and artist statement on pages 95 to 96 and Suzanne's poem on pages 97 to 98.

Read in Remington Review
Exhibit, 2019

Altered Silhouette

Featured with Nancy Smith's painting "Twilight Reflection" at the Women Speak Project Exhibition at the Frank Gallery in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, March 8 to 11, 2019.

Also in Progress

Suspended Silence

Suspended Silence is Suzanne Cottrell's fifth poetry collection, forthcoming from Kelsay Books in 2027. The manuscript is currently being proofed, and Suzanne is gathering endorsements for the collection.

The collection explores:

  • Nature as beauty
  • Nature triggers memories
  • A call to protect the natural world
